About Who Q Me?

Who Q Me? is a cute, spooky detective game about solving your own murder, made by Panduck Studio for Thailand Horror Jam 2026.
Play on itch.io: https://panduck812.itch.io/who-q-me

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: The game is stuck or not responding.
A: Try force-closing and reopening the app. If the problem continues, email support@panduckgame.com with your device model and iOS version.

Q: I found a bug.
A: Email support@panduckgame.com describing what happened, and a screenshot if possible.

Q: Can I suggest a feature or give feedback?
A: Yes — we'd love to hear from you at support@panduckgame.com.
