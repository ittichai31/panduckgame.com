Privacy Policy — Who Q Me?

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Panduck Studio ("we", "us") made Who Q Me?, a short detective game available on the App Store and itch.io.

Who Q Me? does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The game has no user accounts, does not use analytics or advertising services, and does not send or receive player data over the internet. Any progress or settings are stored only on your own device.

Children's Privacy

Who Q Me? is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Third-Party Services

Who Q Me? does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes (for example, if we add online features in the future), we will update this page and change the "Last updated" date above.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@panduckgame.com.
